Catalog

Models AI4BayesCode can build samplers for.

Describe any of these model classes in plain English — with your likelihood, priors, and observed quantities — and AI4BayesCode decomposes it into modular blocks, assigns each block to the right sampling primitive, validates the implementation, and ships a runnable stateful sampler. New model classes are added by writing a short AI Skill and one reference example; see extending the catalog.

Regression & shrinkage

Linear models
Bayesian linear regression

Gaussian-likelihood regression with conjugate or weakly-informative priors on coefficients and residual scale. The canonical workhorse for continuous outcomes.

Powered by: nuts_block · half-Cauchy on σ
GLM
Generalized linear models

Logistic, Poisson, negative-binomial, gamma, beta regression with link functions. Useful when the response is discrete, bounded, or otherwise non-Gaussian.

Powered by: nuts_block · link-aware Jacobians
Shrinkage
Horseshoe regression

Continuous global-local shrinkage on regression coefficients. Strong shrinkage of small effects, near-zero shrinkage of large ones; no inclusion indicators needed.

Powered by: nuts_block on (β, log τ, log λ)
Variable selection
Spike-and-slab regression

Dirac (or normal) spike at zero plus a Gaussian, Laplace, or t slab. Inclusion indicators and coefficients sampled jointly via reversible-jump for trans-dimensional moves.

Powered by: rjmcmc_block · inverse-gamma on σ²
Hierarchical
Mixed-effects models

Fixed effects with grouped random intercepts and slopes, Gaussian or non-Gaussian outcomes. Covers GLMM, longitudinal models, and partial-pooling designs.

Powered by: nuts_block · LKJ on group correlation
Interaction selection
Interaction-aware BVS

Two-layer spike-and-slab over main effects and their pairwise products with a low-dim covariate set, under a weak-hierarchy constraint (interaction only if main is in).

Powered by: rjmcmc_block · Bayesian-lasso scale mixture

Trees & state-space

Tree ensemble
BART

Bayesian additive regression trees for continuous outcomes — flexible nonparametric mean function with built-in shrinkage on tree depth and leaf values.

Powered by: bart_block (stateful tree ensemble)
Generalized trees
Generalized BART

Logistic, Poisson, negative-binomial, and survival BART under the same tree-update primitive. The first publicly available implementation of the generalized BART family.

Powered by: genbart_block · data-augmented latents
Hidden Markov
Hidden Markov models

Discrete-state HMMs with Gaussian, Poisson, or negative-binomial emissions. Ordered emission constraint for identifiability; supports an arbitrary number of states.

Powered by: hmm_block (FFBS) · dirichlet_gibbs on (A, π)
State-space
Linear-Gaussian state-space

Latent continuous state with linear dynamics and Gaussian observations. Kalman filtering for the latent path, NUTS for the hyperparameters.

Powered by: kalman_block · nuts_block on dynamics
Spatio-temporal
Separable GP panel

Multi-channel time series modeled as a separable spatio-temporal Gaussian field: Matérn-in-coordinate tensored with Matérn-in-time, plus independent observation noise.

Powered by: elliptical_slice_block · Kronecker-aware kernel

Spatial & graphical models

Markov random field
Hidden Ising / Potts MRF

Image segmentation and lattice priors: discrete state at each site with a Potts interaction. Cluster updates (Swendsen-Wang) for rapid mixing.

Powered by: ising_cluster_block · Gaussian emission
Disease mapping
BYM2 spatial smoothing

Poisson disease mapping with the BYM2 reparameterization — mixes a structured ICAR component with an unstructured one, with PC priors on the mixing weight.

Powered by: gmrf_precision_block · nuts_block on hyperparams
Stochastic process
Household epidemic dynamics

Closed-population SIR-style epidemic with global and within-household infectious contacts. Intractable likelihood — sampler uses forward simulation.

Powered by: abc_block · simulator-based likelihood
Structure learning
Bayesian network structure

Discrete Bayesian network DAG inference with BDeu or BGe scores. Order-MCMC over node permutations with conjugate parent-set scoring.

Powered by: order_mcmc_block · dirichlet_gibbs on CPTs

Mixtures, clustering, survival

Finite mixture
Gaussian mixture model

Finite mixture of Gaussians with a Dirichlet prior on weights. Latent allocations sampled by conditional Gibbs; component parameters by NUTS.

Powered by: categorical_gibbs · dirichlet_gibbs · nuts_block
Nonparametric
Dirichlet process mixture

Infinite-mixture clustering with a DP prior over partitions. Component count is learned; supports Gaussian, Poisson, or user-defined emissions.

Powered by: dpm_block (slice / Chinese-restaurant)
Survival
Bayesian Cox regression

Proportional-hazards regression with right-censored survival data and a piecewise-constant or BART baseline hazard. Frailty extensions supported.

Powered by: nuts_block · piecewise / bart_block hazard
Survival
Accelerated failure time

AFT model with log-normal, log-logistic, or Weibull baseline and parametric or BART covariate effects. Handles right- and interval-censoring.

Powered by: nuts_block · censoring-aware likelihood

Variational targets

Discrete VI
Categorical mean-field VI on a grid

Coordinate-ascent VI for discrete categorical latents over a 2-D lattice with Potts interaction; closed-form per-site updates given neighbor expectations.

Powered by: categorical_meanfield_vi_block
Structured VI
Structured VI for 3-D Potts

Clique-factorized VI with user-specified clique partition; beats naive mean-field on tightly-coupled lattices and tree-structured factor graphs.

Powered by: structured_meanfield_vi_block
Gaussian VI
Mean-field Gaussian VI

Diagonal-Gaussian variational family with ADVI-style reparameterization. A cheap continuous-VI baseline for any continuous posterior; pairs well with MCMC for sensitivity checks.

Powered by: mean_field_gaussian_vi_block
Full-rank VI
Full-rank Gaussian VI

Low-rank + diagonal Gaussian variational family for high-dimensional posteriors with strong correlation structure. Cheaper than NUTS, more flexible than mean-field.

Powered by: full_rank_gaussian_vi_block
